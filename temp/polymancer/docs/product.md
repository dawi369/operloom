# Polymancer: product

**Bring an idea. Leave with a forecast you can stand behind.**

Polymancer is a thinking partner for people who have views about the world and want to know whether those views hold up. You talk an idea through. Polymancer turns it into a question that can actually be settled, researches both sides, finds the prediction market that really asks it, says honestly whether the market disagrees with the evidence, and keeps a record of how your thinking performed.

## Who it is for

The **idea-holder**: someone who follows politics, the economy, tech and AI, science or world events closely, has opinions they think are good, and rarely or never trades prediction markets.

- They want to know whether their judgment is any good, not to gamble.
- They don't want another feed to watch. They want to be told when something changes their reasoning.
- They are wary of markets that feel like betting, and they need the product to make that distinction for them.

A second audience comes later: active traders who want wallet and cohort signals and live execution (the power-user track in [roadmap.md](roadmap.md)).

## The promise

You leave every session with one of three complete outcomes, and each counts:

1. **A sharper question** with your forecast on record, even if no market asks it.
2. **A price check**: what the evidence says, what the market says, and whether the gap survives costs. "No edge" is an answer.
3. **A paper position** that puts the idea to work with a stated maximum loss, watched quietly until it resolves.

Over weeks, Polymancer gives you something nobody else does: a track record of your own forecasts, scored honestly against what happened.

## Principles

These settle disagreements about scope and design.

1. **Your view comes first.** Polymancer records your forecast before it shows its own estimate or any market price, so the record is genuinely yours.
2. **Three numbers, never blended.** Your forecast, Polymancer's estimate and the market price are always labelled and shown separately.
3. **Argue both sides.** Every brief makes the strongest case for and against, cites dated sources, and states what would change the estimate.
4. **The contract is not your question.** Before any price, Polymancer shows where a market's wording, deadline or resolution rules differ from what you mean.
5. **Honest about edge.** A gap counts only if it survives spread, fees and depth; when it doesn't, Polymancer says so plainly and treats it as a good outcome.
6. **Quiet by default.** Polymancer reaches out only when something material changes your reasoning, or when you asked for a weekly digest.
7. **Paper first, always labelled.** Paper positions are the default and are marked PAPER everywhere; real money is a later, separate opt-in.
8. **Score the thinking, not the payout.** The track record leads with forecast accuracy and calibration; paper profit is secondary.
9. **You decide.** Polymancer estimates and explains; it never tells you to buy, never creates urgency, and never changes your forecast for you.

## What Polymancer is not

- A tip service or signal seller.
- A market browser, odds feed or leaderboard.
- A sportsbook. Sports markets are out of scope.
- A wallet or custodian. Polymancer never holds user funds.

## Why it can win

Research on the [planning map](https://github.com/dawi369/polymancer/issues/4) (competition ticket "Who competes for the idea-holder, and is there room for a solo developer?") found the following. This is a dated snapshot from 2026-10-03 of what each product publicly offered, not a permanent fact:

| Who                                     | What they do                                         | What they leave open                                                        |
| --------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Alphascope                              | Question → contract match → AI forecast versus price | Stops at research: no interview, no position, no follow-up, no track record |
| Polyseer                                | Deep research reports on a market                    | No interview, no positions, no monitoring                                   |
| Robinhood, Public with Kalshi, Coinbase | AI agents that act inside brokerage accounts         | Execution-first; nobody interviews a fuzzy idea or argues both sides        |
| Google, ChatGPT, Grok                   | Show market odds as information                      | No loop at all                                                              |
| Copy-trading bots                       | Mirror wallets, often with custodial keys            | Trader-only, opaque, custody risk                                           |

Unclaimed, and Polymancer's job:

- interviewing a non-trader's fuzzy idea into a testable question;
- arguing both sides with sources;
- checking contract rule fit;
- a novice-friendly paper position;
- watching it against the original reasoning;
- an honest calibration record afterwards.

The defensible asset is the record. Incumbents can add a chat box; they will not keep a user's dated reasoning and score it over months.

## Business model

| Tier | Price (hypothesis)     | For                                                                                               |
| ---- | ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Free | $0                     | A few questions a month with standard research, paper positions, weekly check-in, the ChatGPT app |
| Plus | about $9–12/month      | More questions and deep research, daily watch, alerts, full track record, call cards              |
| Pro  | about $29/month, later | Live trading (each trade approved) where eligible, and the power-user track                       |

- Deep research beyond the monthly allowance is pay-per-use. A standard brief costs about $0.03 and a deep brief about $0.50 in provider fees.
- Signup and upgrades happen on the web.
- There are no performance fees and no per-trade markup.
- Venue builder fees exist only on real orders, so paper trading earns nothing from venues. Subscriptions are the business.

## Success measures

- **Activation**: a new user saves a question with their forecast in the first session.
- **Trust**: share of price checks that end in "no edge" without the user leaving. People should accept honest answers.
- **Return**: users who come back to a watched question after a watch alert, and on resolution day.
- **Record**: users with five or more resolved questions after 90 days.
- **Conversion**: Free to Plus after the first resolved question.

The weakest assumption is that non-traders value a slow, honest record enough to return. [roadmap.md](roadmap.md) describes the one-month cohort test that checks it first.
